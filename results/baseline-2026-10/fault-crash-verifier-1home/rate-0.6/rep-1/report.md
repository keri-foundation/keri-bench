## Summary

This run offered 0.6 credentials a second for 58.3 s (repetition 1), and 36 credential(s) were requested in its measured window.
The harness kept to its own schedule throughout.
From request to `verified`, the median credential took 3.14 s and 1 in 100 took longer than 4.82 s, over 36 credential(s).

There are no warnings.
